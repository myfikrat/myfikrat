Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

fotook.net (“fotook”, “we”, “us”) provides free photo and PDF tools that run entirely in your web browser. This policy explains what data we do and do not handle when you use the site.

The short version: Your photos and PDFs are processed locally on your own device. For the free tools, your files are never uploaded to any server and we cannot see them.

1. Files you process with the free tools

All of our free tools — resizing and compressing images, checking DV Lottery / Schengen / passport photos, merging, splitting, organizing, editing and converting PDFs, and scanning documents — run completely inside your browser using JavaScript. The files you open:

  • are read and processed only in your device’s memory;
  • are never transmitted to us or any third party;
  • are discarded as soon as you close or reload the page.

2. Optional paid AI photo check

The only feature that sends a file off your device is the optional, paid “full AI photo report.” If you choose to purchase it, the specific photo you submit is sent securely to our processing server (and to our AI provider, Anthropic) solely to generate your report. It is used only for that request and is not stored afterwards or used for any other purpose. If you never buy this feature, no file ever leaves your device.

3. Google Drive & Dropbox import

You may optionally import files from Google Drive or Dropbox. If you do:

  • You sign in through Google’s or Dropbox’s own secure screens. We never see your password.
  • We request the minimum access needed to let you pick and download the file you choose. We only read the single file you select — we do not browse, modify, or store the rest of your Drive or Dropbox.
  • The file you pick is downloaded straight into your browser and processed locally, exactly like a file from your computer.
  • Access tokens live only in your browser tab for that session and are discarded when you close it. We do not retain them.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Payments

Payments for the optional AI report are handled by PayPal. We never see or store your card or PayPal login details — PayPal processes the payment and only tells us whether it succeeded. See PayPal’s own privacy policy for how they handle your data.

5. What we collect about you

  • No account required. You can use every free tool without signing up.
  • No advertising trackers and no selling of data — ever.
  • Our host may keep standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to prevent abuse. These are not linked to your files.

6. Data security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because free-tool processing happens on your own device, there is no file store to breach. The optional paid photo check is transmitted over an encrypted connection and processed transiently.

7. Children

fotook is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top will always reflect the current version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@fotook.net.
